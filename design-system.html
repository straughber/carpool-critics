9. Do / Don't

DO

  • Space with the scale; group with distance, not a border.
  • One amber primary per view.
  • Give every async surface empty, loading and error states.
  • Let a missing number keep its cell and read --/--.

DON'T

  • Add a sixth nav item instead of finding the link's home.
  • Centre a card in a field of asphalt.
  • Use opacity as a state.
  • Animate a state change, a card entry, or a list reveal.

10. Contrast floor

Body 4.5:1 minimum, large text 3.0:1 at 24px or larger, non-text (borders, focus rings, gauge cells) 3.0:1. Measured from the palette by tools/design_system.py, not quoted from this page.

13. Interference

    

      A state signal, not a texture. Every effect names a condition from the ladder;
      the generated layer is assets/css/interference.css and the burst API is
      Interference.burst(element, effect, frames). Reduced motion freezes
      each effect on its representative frame.
    

    

      
StateMeaningAllowedDuration

      
        
idleNothing wrongscanlines onlystatic

        
degradedReadable but weakvstripes, roll, noise, tear≤ 3 frames, max 1 / 4s

        
lostPicture gonenoise, roll, headswitchhold; still mandatory

        
fatalFailure with an actionnoise, tearone burst, then still
