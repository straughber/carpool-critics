About Curbside Cinema

Broadcasting live from the cabin of a BMW X5 overlooking the Austin night skyline. Curbside Cinema brings raw, unfiltered movie debriefs the second the credits roll.

Driver Seat

Shane

Behind the wheel in the Columbia blue fishing shirt. Breaks down cinematography, camera angles, pacing, and the mechanical execution of modern cinema.

Center Back Seat

Alex

Holding court between the front seats in the maroon striped shirt. Dissects narrative structure, thematic depth, character dynamics, and psychological thrillers.

Passenger Seat

Dave

Riding shotgun in the graphic tee and black cap. Resident franchise historian and champion of practical monster effects with an encyclopedic memory of 80s and 90s cinema.
