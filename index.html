About Carpool Cinema Critics

Broadcasting live from the cabin of a BMW X5 overlooking the Austin night skyline. Carpool Cinema Critics brings raw, unfiltered movie debriefs the second the credits roll.

Driver Seat

Alex

Behind the wheel in the maroon striped shirt. Dissects screenplay pacing, narrative arcs, and psychological thrillers with razor-sharp insight.

Center Back Seat

Dave

Holding court between the front seats in the white graphic tee and black cap. The resident franchise historian and creature feature champion with encyclopedic cinema memory.

Passenger Seat

Shane

Riding shotgun in the Columbia blue fishing shirt. Breaks down cinematography, lighting choices, framing, and the technical mechanics of modern film.
