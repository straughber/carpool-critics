About Carpool Cinema Critics

Three dudes who hit up pre-screenings and hang out in the parking lot to unpack hot takes, have plenty of laughs, and give incorrect opinions.

Driver Seat

Alex

He channels his background in writing into a deep appreciation for strong storytelling and character arcs. Whether he is unpacking complex narratives or celebrating the finer, eye-opening documentaries in life, he brings thoughtful analysis and sharp wit to the front seat.

Back Seat

Dave

The man, the myth, the legend, leverages a background in tech to appreciate smart execution in cinema. Despite his analytical side, he carries a massive soft spot for a great rom-com, bringing infectious optimism with a side of love for happily ever after.

Passenger Seat

Shane

With a background in design, marketing, and sports nutrition, he's always appreciating the details and is a total sucker for a good coming-of-age story.
